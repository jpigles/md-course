Textbooks

  • Understanding Molecular Simulation: From Algorithms to ApplicationsDaan Frenkel, Berend Smit · 3rd ed., 2023 · Academic Press (Elsevier)The standard algorithm-level reference connecting statistical mechanics to working MD, free-energy and rare-event methods.Where to read: Ch. 2 Thermodynamics and Statistical Mechanics; Ch. 4 Molecular Dynamics Simulations; Ch. 5 Computer Experiments; Ch. 7 Molecular Dynamics in Various Ensembles; Ch. 8 Free Energy Calculations; Ch. 11 Long-Ranged Interactions; Ch. 15 Rare Events; Appendix G Statistical Errors; Appendix H Integration Schemes
  • Computer Simulation of LiquidsMichael P. Allen, Dominic J. Tildesley · 2nd ed., 2017 · Oxford University PressClassic practical MD/MC text with an official companion site of Fortran and Python example programs (also on GitHub).
  • Statistical Mechanics: Theory and Molecular SimulationMark E. Tuckerman · 2nd ed., 2023 · Oxford University PressRigorous derivation of ensembles, extended-system thermostats/barostats, reversible integrators and enhanced sampling from Hamiltonian mechanics.Where to read: Ch. 17 covers machine learning (new in the 2nd edition).
  • Molecular Modelling: Principles and ApplicationsAndrew R. Leach · 2nd ed., 2001 · Prentice Hall (Pearson)Broad, readable introduction to force fields, energy minimisation, MD and Monte Carlo with biomolecular examples.
  • The Art of Molecular Dynamics SimulationD. C. Rapaport · 2nd ed., 2004 · Cambridge University PressCase-study book that builds MD programs step by step, good for students who want to write their own code.
  • Molecular Modeling and Simulation: An Interdisciplinary GuideTamar Schlick · 2nd ed., 2010 · SpringerBiomolecule-focused text covering force fields, minimisation, MD integration and long-range electrostatics with a mathematical flavour.
  • Molecular Dynamics: With Deterministic and Stochastic Numerical MethodsBen Leimkuhler, Charles Matthews · 2015 · SpringerThe mathematically careful treatment of symplectic integrators, Langevin dynamics, thermostats and constraints.
  • Introduction to Modern Statistical MechanicsDavid Chandler · 1987 · Oxford University PressShort, clear first course in statistical mechanics aimed at chemists, including Monte Carlo and time correlation functions.
  • Statistical Physics of Biomolecules: An IntroductionDaniel M. Zuckerman · 1st ed., 2010 · CRC Press (Taylor & Francis)Biomolecule-oriented statistical mechanics with a dedicated chapter on sampling and simulation, pitched at life-science students.Where to read: Ch. 12, A Statistical Perspective on Biomolecular Simulation.
  • Molecular Driving Forces: Statistical Thermodynamics in Biology, Chemistry, Physics, and NanoscienceKen A. Dill, Sarina Bromberg · 2nd ed., 2010 · Garland Science (Taylor & Francis)Intuitive, example-driven statistical thermodynamics for biophysics students with little physics background.
  • Free Energy Calculations: Theory and Applications in Chemistry and BiologyChristophe Chipot, Andrew Pohorille (eds.) · 2007 · SpringerEdited volume with the theory behind FEP, TI, umbrella sampling and related methods, written by the method developers.
  • Statistical MechanicsDonald A. McQuarrie · 2000 · University Science BooksThorough classical reference for ensembles, partition functions and liquid-state theory.
  • Geometric Numerical Integration: Structure-Preserving Algorithms for Ordinary Differential EquationsErnst Hairer, Christian Lubich, Gerhard Wanner · 2nd ed., 2006 · SpringerDefinitive mathematics of symplectic and time-reversible integrators and backward error analysis, the theory behind velocity Verlet.
  • Classical MechanicsHerbert Goldstein, Charles P. Poole, John L. Safko · 3rd ed., 2002 · Pearson / Addison-WesleyStandard graduate source for Lagrangian and Hamiltonian mechanics and canonical transformations.
  • Statistical Mechanics: Entropy, Order Parameters, and Complexity freeJames P. Sethna · 2nd ed., 2021 · Oxford University Press (free PDF hosted by the author)Modern, computation-friendly statistical mechanics text with a legal free PDF on the author's page.
